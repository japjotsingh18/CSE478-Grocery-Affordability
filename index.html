Can Students Still Afford the Basics?

An Interactive Visual Story of Food Prices and the Student Grocery Budget

This project explores how grocery prices in the U.S. have changed from 2016 - 2026 as well as how these changes can affect college students. We are starting by comparing the prices of 3 common grocery items:- eggs, milk, and bread.

About the Data

  • Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS), accessed through FRED.
  • Items: Eggs, milk, and white bread.
  • Time Period: January 2016 – October 2026.
  • Dataset: 387 monthly price records.
  • Missing Data: October 2025 prices were unavailable, so those values were left out.
Data Sources (FRED, from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics): Eggs (APU0000708111) · Milk (APU0000709112) · Bread (APU0000702111)
